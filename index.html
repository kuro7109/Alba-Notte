Nestled in the heart of Malta, we're a place shaped by sunlight, shared tables, and long conversations. Inspired by travel, local produce, and the rhythms of island life.

Visit Us

Born from a journey across cultures, our space celebrates what Malta does best — honest food, warm weather, and time well spent.

By day, we serve fresh coffee, slow breakfasts, and light Mediterranean plates inspired by Maltese bread, sun-ripened tomatoes, and seasonal fruits.

As the sun sets, the lights dim, the music shifts, and the space transforms into an intimate bar — serving thoughtful cocktails, local wines, and shared plates designed for the night.

One place. Two moods. Always Malta.

When the Island Slows Down

As daylight fades, the café evolves into a bar designed for connection.

Low lighting. Warm tones. Conversations that stretch longer than planned.

At night, we offer:

  • Signature cocktails inspired by Mediterranean ingredients
  • Italian and local wines
  • Small plates made for sharing
  • A relaxed, intimate atmosphere — not loud, not rushed

This is not nightlife.

This is night, done right.

A flaky, golden croissant inspired by the layered technique of North Indian laccha paratha, laminated with a butter–ghee blend for added richness and aroma. Served warm with white butter and jaggery or honey with salted butter, allowing a balance of sweetness and depth.

Tandoori Paneer & Spiced Tomato Bruschetta

€9.50

Olive-oil toasted sourdough layered with a slow-cooked spiced tomato base inspired by Indian tomato bhuna, finished with lightly charred tandoori paneer. Balanced with pickled onions and fresh herbs, this savoury open toast blends Indian warmth with Italian simplicity.

Bhetki “Malai Bianco” Pasta

€15.50

Tagliatelle tossed in a creamy white sauce inspired by Bengali malai, built with butter, cream, and gentle aromatics. Soft flakes of pan-seared bhetki (sea bass) are folded through the pasta and finished with white pepper and a touch of citrus, blending coastal Indian richness with Italian pasta tradition.
